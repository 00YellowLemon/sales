# Enterprise Sales Demo Skill

## Overview
This skill outlines how to craft a highly effective product demo. Instead of walking through features, a great demo tells a personalized story that directly solves the problems uncovered during the qualification call.

## Demo Design Workflow
1. **Adopt the Correct Mindset**
   - A demo is NOT a chance to show off all your product's features.
   - It is an opportunity to convince the prospect that you can solve their specific problem.

2. **Use the "Great Movie Script" Approach**
   - Think of the demo as a movie script.
   - Start by recapping who the main character is (the prospect/user) and the problem they are trying to solve.
   - This builds credibility by proving you listened during the first call.

3. **Avoid the Feature Tour**
   - Do not walk screen to screen showing everything the product can do.

4. **Tell a Personalized Story**
   - Show exactly how the main character solves their problem using the software.
   - Every feature shown must have a clear reason for being there that ties back to the prospect's problem.
   - *Personalization*: Use the information gathered in the first call to tailor the demo. Incorporate the prospect's logo, website, customers, and team member names. Help them visualize exactly how the product fits *their* company.

5. **Create Magic Moments**
   - Identify points in the workflow to surprise the audience with how easy or delightful a solution is (e.g., showing changes live on the customer's actual website instead of a dummy site).
