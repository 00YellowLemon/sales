# YC's Essential Startup Advice: Growth & Fundraising

**Source:** [YC's essential startup advice](https://www.ycombinator.com/library/4D-yc-s-essential-startup-advice)

## Prerequisites for Growth
- A startup without growth is usually a failure, but *how* and *when* to grow is often misunderstood.
- YC pushes companies to talk to users, build what they want, and iterate. Growth is a natural result of doing these successfully.
- **Product Market Fit (PMF) Rule:** If you have not yet made something customers want (i.e., no PMF), it makes zero sense to push for growth. Poor retention is always the result.

## Unit Economics
- **The Profitability Rule:** If you have an unprofitable product, growth merely drains cash from the company.
- As Paul Buchheit says: "It never makes sense to take 80 cents from a customer and then hand them a dollar back."
- Unit economics *really* matter; many startups forget this basic fact.

## Choosing Success Metrics
- One of the hardest things in a startup is choosing what to do.
- **The Metric Rule:** Very early on, choose one or two key metrics to measure success.
- Founders should choose what tasks to work on based nearly exclusively on how the task will impact those metrics.

## Fundraising Mindset
- **Speed:** Raise money as quickly as possible and then get back to work. A company's growth curve often flattens out while fundraising because of the distraction.
- **Valuation $\neq$ Success:** Valuation is not equal to success or even the probability of success. Great companies (Airbnb, Dropbox, Twitch) raised on tiny initial valuations.
- **Fiduciary Duty:** The money you raise is NOT your money. Founders have an ethical/moral duty to spend the money only to improve the prospects of the company.

## Founder Wellbeing & Cofounder Relationships
- Most companies fail fast because founders fall out. The relationship with your cofounder matters more than you think. Open, honest communication prevents debacles.
- **Be Nice:** Being nice is one of the best things you can do for startup success (Mean People Fail).
- **Sanity:** Take breaks, spend time with family, sleep, and exercise in between bouts of intense focus.
