# Sales Advisor Agent - Responsibilities and Tasks

Based on the Sales Playbook, this document outlines the high-level strategies and granular advisory actions that a Sales Advisor Agent would be responsible for. The Advisor Agent's role is to answer questions, provide strategic guidance, review materials, and help the founder navigate B2B sales challenges based on their current stage.

## High-Level Advisory Phases

*   **Phase 1: Early Customer Discovery & Strategy Formulation**
    *   Advise against entering long, poorly defined unpaid design partnerships.
    *   Help refine discovery questions to uncover narrow, burning problems.
    *   Provide guidance on defining and scoping a "wedge" product strategy.
*   **Phase 2: Navigating Pilots & Proof of Concepts (POCs)**
    *   Review and provide feedback on proposed "Value Equations" to ensure they are measurable and compelling.
    *   Recommend specific risk-lowering techniques (e.g., backtesting, volume limiting) based on the customer's profile.
    *   Coach the founder on how to initiate "willingness to pay" conversations without fear.
*   **Phase 3: Transitioning to Paid Engagements**
    *   Advise on strategies to secure upfront financial commitments for pilots.
    *   Provide tactics to bypass lengthy procurement processes (e.g., utilizing corporate card limits).
    *   Review prerequisites and timing to ensure optimal pilot success and high engagement.
*   **Phase 4: Structuring Recurring Revenue Contracts ("The Pro Move")**
    *   Explain the mechanics and benefits of transitioning directly to recurring contracts with opt-out clauses.
    *   Help formulate positioning statements to confidently present standard contract terms to new prospects.
*   **Phase 5: Post-Sale Customer Success & Operations**
    *   Advise on the necessity and structure of a dedicated customer success function.
    *   Provide guidance on when and how to initiate security certifications like SOC 2.
    *   Help map the buying organization and strategize on winning over internal stakeholders.

## Granular Advisory Actions & Tasks

### Early Customer Discovery
*   [ ] Review and critique proposed discovery questions (e.g., verify they focus on pain points like "What part of your job do you hate most?").
*   [ ] Analyze founder feedback from observation sessions to help identify potential "wedge" solutions.
*   [ ] Provide pushback if the founder is tempted to build custom features for a non-paying partner.
*   [ ] Advise on strategies to find and pitch 10 similar customers once a wedge is validated.

### Pilots and Proof of Concepts
*   [ ] Evaluate the proposed "Value Equation" for a pilot (e.g., checking if projected ROI or time saved is clear and quantifiable).
*   [ ] Suggest the most appropriate risk mitigation tactic for a specific cautious buyer scenario.
*   [ ] Roleplay or review scripts for "willingness to pay" conversations.
*   [ ] Remind the founder to book post-pilot review meetings before starting the pilot.

### Execution of Paid Pilots
*   [ ] Advise on appropriate upfront pricing structures for paid pilots.
*   [ ] Brainstorm strategies to find and utilize an internal champion's unapproved spending limit.
*   [ ] Review the list of prerequisites before a pilot begins to ensure maximum engagement.
*   [ ] Suggest "janky" or non-scalable processes to drastically reduce "Time to First Value" (e.g., manual Excel imports instead of API integrations).

### Contracting and Closing
*   [ ] Review draft contract terms to ensure they feature recurring structures with opt-out clauses (e.g., 30-60 days).
*   [ ] Help the founder map the buying organization and identify missing stakeholders (economic buyer, technical approver, etc.).
*   [ ] Evaluate legal redlines to identify which terms are truly "company-ending" versus minor annoyances.
*   [ ] Draft or review "scarcity" emails to create urgency with hesitant buyers.
*   [ ] Advise on the timing and strategy of in-person travel to close critical deals.

### Post-Sale and Operations
*   [ ] Review proposed customer onboarding schedules and check-in plans.
*   [ ] Advise on the urgency of starting SOC 2 or other necessary security certifications.
*   [ ] Provide guidance on communicating MRR/ARR to investors correctly when opt-out periods are active.

## Required Founder Information

Before the Advisor Agent can provide effective, tailored advice, it needs to gather the following context from the founder:

*   **Current Sales Stage:** Where is the founder currently in the sales progression? (e.g., Ideation, Design Partnership, Free Pilot, Paid Pilot, Closing Recurring Contracts).
*   **Product Readiness:** Is the product a raw idea, a 48-hour "wedge" build, or a fully functional application?
*   **Target Customer Profile:** Who are they selling to? (e.g., Industry, company size, typical buyer persona like CFO vs. Developer).
*   **Current Bottlenecks/Fears:** What is the specific challenge the founder is facing? (e.g., Fear of asking for money, low pilot engagement, legal redlines).
*   **Deal Mechanics:** What is the typical deal size, and what is the proposed "Value Equation" for the customer?
*   **Customer Engagement Level:** How engaged is the current prospect? Are they dedicating resources to the pilot, or is the founder doing all the work?
