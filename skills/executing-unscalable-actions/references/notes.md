# YC's Essential Startup Advice: Unscalable Actions

**Source:** [YC's essential startup advice](https://www.ycombinator.com/library/4D-yc-s-essential-startup-advice)

## Do Things That Don't Scale
- Once launched, founders must do things that don't scale.
- **The Scaling Trap:** Advisors often push startups to scale prematurely, building technology and processes too early. This is a waste of time and often leads to startup death.
- **The Mandate:** Get the first customer by *any means necessary*. This includes manual work that cannot be managed for 10 users, let alone 100 or 1,000.
- Founders are still figuring out what to build; talking directly to customers during unscalable manual tasks is the best way to learn.
- **Airbnb Example:** Founders manually photographed early listings to improve conversion and had amazing customer conversations in the process.

## Customer Selection & Firing
- Founders often don't realize they can choose their customers.
- **The Rule of 10:** Recruiting 10 customers who have a *burning problem* is much better than 1,000 customers who have a *passing annoyance*.
- **Firing Customers:** It is sometimes critical to fire customers. Some cost more than they provide in revenue or learning.
- **Twitch Example:** Justin.tv/Twitch became a breakout success only when they focused entirely on video game broadcasters and fired people trying to stream copyrighted content.

## Focus on the Acute Problem
- When an early product isn't working, it's tempting to build new features to solve every possible problem.
- Instead, talk to the customer and focus *only* on the most acute problem they have. Do less, really well.
