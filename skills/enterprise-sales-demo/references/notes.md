# Enterprise Sales Demo Notes

*Based on Y Combinator's Startup School talk by Pete Koomen*

## Step 4: Delivering a Great Demo
- **Mindset Shift:** A demo is not a chance to show off your product. It is a chance to convince the audience that you can help them solve their problem.
- **The "Great Movie Script" Approach:**
  - Think of the demo as the script for a great movie.
  - Start by recapping who the main character is (the user) and the problem she is trying to solve.
  - This demonstrates how well you were listening during the first call. If the audience believes you understand their company and problems, they will take you seriously when you talk about solutions.
- **Avoid the Feature Tour:** Do not walk screen to screen showing everything the product can do.
- **Tell a Story:** Show exactly how the main character solves her problem. Demos should have a flow where each step leads to the next, and every feature shown has a clear reason for being there.
- **Magic Moments:** Surprise your audience with how easy or delightful something is.
- **Personalization is Critical:**
  - Use the information collected during the first call.
  - Tailor the demo to their company: Use their logo, their website, their customers, and the names of the people on their team. Help them visualize exactly how the product would work in *their* company.
  - *Example (Optimizely):* Instead of using a dummy website to show A/B testing (which competitors did and was "lame"), they spent weeks building a feature that let them demo the product directly on the *customer's live website*. Marketers' eyes "lit up" when they saw things changed on their own landing page that would have taken months to do on their own.
