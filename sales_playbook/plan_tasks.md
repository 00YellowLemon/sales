# Sales Planner Agent - Responsibilities and Tasks

Based on the Sales Playbook, this document outlines the high-level strategies and granular actions that a Sales Planner Agent would be responsible for scheduling, managing, and guiding a founder through, depending on the current stage of their startup and sales progress.

## High-Level Phases

*   **Phase 1: Early Customer Discovery & The Wedge Strategy**
    *   Identify narrow, burning problems through direct observation.
    *   Build and test a "wedge" product aggressively.
    *   Avoid long, poorly defined unpaid design partnerships.
*   **Phase 2: Short-Term Pilots & Proof of Concepts (POCs)**
    *   Structure defined, metric-driven POCs to prove value equations.
    *   Utilize risk-lowering techniques for cautious buyers (backtesting, side-by-side trials).
    *   Establish willingness to pay early on.
*   **Phase 3: Paid Pilots & Rapid Iteration**
    *   Secure financial commitment for pilots to increase customer engagement.
    *   Shorten pilot lengths (e.g., 7 to 14 days) and prioritize rapid bug fixes.
    *   Optimize "Time to First Value" using whatever means necessary (even non-scalable ones).
*   **Phase 4: The "Pro Move" - Recurring Contracts with Opt-Outs**
    *   Transition from paid pilots to default-alive recurring revenue contracts with initial grace/opt-out periods (e.g., 30-60 days).
    *   Streamline the sales process by eliminating the post-pilot negotiation phase.
*   **Phase 5: Post-Sale Customer Success & Operations**
    *   Dedicate resources to onboarding and ensuring customers realize product value.
    *   Initiate security certifications (SOC 2, HIPAA) proactively.
    *   Map the buying organization and cultivate internal champions.

## Granular Actions & Tasks

### Early Customer Discovery
*   [ ] Draft discovery questions focused on current pain points (e.g., "What part of your job do you hate most?").
*   [ ] Schedule observation sessions to shadow target customers while they work.
*   [ ] Define a 48-hour build scope for a narrow "wedge" solution.
*   [ ] Create a target list of 10 similar customers to pitch the newly validated wedge product.
*   [ ] Set limits/reminders to prevent building customized features beyond the wedge for a single non-paying partner.

### Pilots and Proof of Concepts
*   [ ] Define the "Value Equation" for the pilot (e.g., projected ROI, time saved, revenue uplift).
*   [ ] Determine the specific metric/sample size needed to prove the Value Equation (e.g., 1,000 resolved queries).
*   [ ] Select a risk mitigation tactic to implement (e.g., historical backtesting, side-by-side run, volume limiting).
*   [ ] Draft the "willingness to pay" conversation script (e.g., "If I deliver X metric, what is that worth?").
*   [ ] Schedule the post-pilot review meeting *before* the pilot officially kicks off.

### Execution of Paid Pilots
*   [ ] Determine the upfront cost/price for the paid pilot.
*   [ ] Identify the internal champion's maximum unapproved spending limit (e.g., $10k on a corporate card) to bypass procurement.
*   [ ] Verify prerequisites are met before starting the pilot (e.g., data is ready, the client team is assembled, a live project is starting).
*   [ ] Schedule rapid check-in calls (every 2-3 days) during the 7-14 day pilot window.
*   [ ] Identify non-scalable/janky processes (e.g., manual Excel imports) that can bypass long integration times and decrease "Time to First Value."

### Contracting and Closing
*   [ ] Draft the standard contract template featuring a recurring subscription structure and a 30-day or 60-day opt-out clause.
*   [ ] Map the customer's buying organization: identify the economic buyer, technical approver, security gatekeeper, and legal team.
*   [ ] Assign specific engagement tactics for each mapped stakeholder.
*   [ ] Schedule in-person travel/visits for critical deals nearing the finish line.
*   [ ] Review legal redlines to quickly concede on any term that is not "company-ending."
*   [ ] Draft a "scarcity" email/message to push a hesitant buyer toward a commitment.

### Post-Sale and Operations
*   [ ] Create a customer onboarding schedule.
*   [ ] Schedule regular check-ins to monitor usage and customer success post-sale.
*   [ ] Initiate the SOC 2 (or equivalent) certification process with a provider like Vanta.
