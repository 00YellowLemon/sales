# YC's Essential Startup Advice: Launch Readiness

**Source:** [YC's essential startup advice](https://www.ycombinator.com/library/4D-yc-s-essential-startup-advice)

## Launching Immediately
- The very first advice YC gives founders is to launch their product right away.
- Launching immediately is the only way to fully understand customer problems and assess if the product meets their needs.
- **Counter-intuitive rule:** Launching a mediocre product ASAP, talking to customers, and iterating is fundamentally better than waiting to build the "perfect" product.
- **Quantum of Utility:** A product can be launched early as long as it contains a "quantum of utility" — a core value that is strong enough to outweigh the friction of any bugs or missing features.

## The 90/10 Solution
- Founders often get a long, complicated list of requested features after talking to early users.
- YC Partner Paul Buchheit (PB) advises looking for the "90/10 solution".
- **Definition:** Finding a way to accomplish 90% of what the user wants with only 10% of the work/effort/time.
- If founders search hard, a 90/10 solution is almost always available.
- **Decision Rule:** A 90% solution to a real customer problem that can be delivered right away is always superior to a 100% solution that takes a long time to build.

## Brokenness is Normal
- Founders shouldn't worry if their company or product feels badly broken at the start.
- Nearly every startup, including future billion-dollar companies, has deep fundamental issues early on.
- Success is not defined by being unbroken at the start, but by how founders address the inevitable, continuous problems.
- The founder's job will constantly feel like righting a capsized ship.
