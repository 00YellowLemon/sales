# Enterprise Sales Qualification Skill

## Overview
This skill provides instructions for handling the all-important first call (qualification call) in the enterprise sales process. It emphasizes listening over pitching, humanizing the sales process, and uncovering true customer problems and budgets.

## Qualification Workflow (The First Call)
1. **Adopt the Correct Mindset**
   - **Primary Rule**: Your job on the first call is NOT to sell the product. Do not dive straight into a pitch.
   - You are selling to an *individual human*, not a monolithic entity.
   - Sales is not adversarial; it's about deeply understanding a problem and helping solve it.

2. **Execute Qualification Goals**
   - **Goal 1**: Figure out whether the prospect has the problem you're solving, the budget to pay for a solution, and the decision-making authority to buy it.
   - **Goal 2**: If qualified, schedule a follow-up call for a product demo.

3. **Listen More Than You Pitch**
   - Spend the majority of the call listening.

4. **Ask Crucial Qualification Questions**
   - Advise the user to ask questions like:
     - What made you decide to take this call?
     - Tell me about this problem.
     - How long have you had it?
     - How bad is it?
     - Who else does it affect?
     - How do you quantify the impact?
     - Why haven't you solved it already?
     - What's your budget for solving it?
     - How does your organization buy software?
     - Who makes the buying decision?
     - Who else will need to weigh in on this decision?

5. **Evaluate the Outcome**
   - If they don't have the problem, don't care, or lack a budget, *this is a great outcome*. It saves time.
   - If they have a solvable problem, they have earned the right to see a demo.
