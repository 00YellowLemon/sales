# Enterprise Sales Closing Skill

## Overview
This skill covers the critical final stages of the enterprise sales funnel: Pricing, Closing (navigating procurement), and Managing Implementation. It helps founders avoid the common pitfalls that cause seemingly "done" deals to fall through or fail to renew.

## Pricing Workflow
1. **Leverage Qualification Data**
   - Use answers from the qualification call to guide pricing (cost of the problem, budget, spend on competitors).
2. **Treat Pricing as an Experiment**
   - There is no single formula. Pick a number (ideally one that makes you slightly uncomfortable) and observe the reaction.
   - *Tip*: Do not publish enterprise tier pricing to allow flexibility on calls.
3. **Avoid Pricing Too Low**
   - High prices help validate if customers actually need the product and makes them take the implementation more seriously.
   - Do not make the product free out of fear.
4. **Enable Your Champion**
   - Provide a PDF one-pager or slides explaining the pricing, product overview, and benefits. The Champion needs this for internal conversations.

## Closing (Procurement) Workflow
1. **Uncover the Buying Process Upfront**
   - Ask early: "How do you buy software?" and "Who needs to sign off?"
   - Do not assume a deal is done just because the Champion says "yes".
2. **Accelerate Procurement**
   - Start parallel processes early (e.g., security questionnaires).
   - Keep legal documents simple (consider YC Common Paper templates).
   - Keep timelines and scope of work *out* of the legal contract; put them in a separate shared order form or project tracker.
3. **Leverage Your Champion**
   - Maintain constant communication. When stuck in procurement (legal, security), ask the Champion for help since they are incentivized to get the solution.

## Implementation Workflow
1. **Manage the Implementation (Do NOT leave it to the customer)**
   - The customer bought a *solution*, not just a product. It is your job to ensure they use it.
2. **Plan Early**
   - Begin asking leaders (e.g., engineering) about implementation requirements early in the sales process.
   - Build a detailed implementation plan *before* signing a contract.
3. **Project Manage It**
   - Create a shared roadmap with the customer.
   - Assign owners to every task.
   - Set up regular check-in meetings for accountability.
4. **Define the True End of the Funnel**
   - The funnel ends when the customer uses the product habitually, not when the contract is signed.
