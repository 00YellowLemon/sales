# Startup Metrics Advisor Agent - Responsibilities and Required Information

Based on the Startup Metrics playbook, this document outlines the required information the founder must provide and the strategic advisory tasks the Metrics Advisor Agent will perform. The advisor acts to review the founder's work, providing critical guidance and ensuring best practices are followed.

## Required Information

Context the founder must provide for the advisor agent to effectively review and advise.

### Pre-Launch Tooling & Definition
* [ ] The 4 or 5 key metrics selected for initial tracking.
* [ ] Precise, company-wide definitions for the selected key metrics.
* [ ] Current tooling used to track metrics (e.g., SQL, PostHog).
* [ ] Details of big decisions currently being tested (e.g., pricing strategy).
* [ ] Frequency and methods of talking to customers directly.

### Investor Reporting & Fundamentals
* [ ] Current Revenue numbers (excluding rebates or cashback).
* [ ] Metrics currently included in key performance dashboards (identifying any vanity metrics like GMV, page views).
* [ ] Current Net Burn Rate (monthly costs minus revenues).
* [ ] Total bank balance.
* [ ] Current calculated Runway (total bank balance divided by net burn rate).

### Retention & Cohort Analysis
* [ ] Monthly retention rates for paying customer cohorts.
* [ ] Visualized retention curves or "layer cake" cohort graphs.
* [ ] Calculated Net Dollar Retention (NDR) for the past 12 months.
* [ ] Customer feedback or interview insights regarding product usage and churn.

### Unit Economics Optimization
* [ ] Detailed Cost of Goods Sold (COGS), including AWS bills, bandwidth, and API credits.
* [ ] True future cost of AI foundation models (ignoring free startup API credits).
* [ ] Calculated Gross Margin (Revenue minus COGS).
* [ ] Current unit economics per customer (identifying positive or negative margins).
* [ ] Proposed roadmap for flipping negative unit economics to profitability, if applicable.

---

## Advisory Tasks

Strategic guidance and review tasks the agent provides to the founder.

### Pre-Launch Tooling & Definition
* [ ] Review the 4 or 5 selected key metrics to ensure they align with testing big decisions rather than minor details.
* [ ] Verify that metric definitions are strict, clear, and consistent over time to avoid internal misalignment.
* [ ] Advise against relying on vanity metrics or building overly complex dashboards with hundreds of metrics pre-launch.
* [ ] Review the founder's efforts to maintain direct contact with customers, ensuring they are not hiding behind metrics.

### Investor Reporting & Fundamentals
* [ ] Advise the founder to prioritize Revenue as the primary key metric over vanity metrics like GMV, GTV, or page views.
* [ ] Review investor update drafts to ensure Revenue, Net Burn Rate, and Runway are prominently featured at the top.
* [ ] Advise the founder on honest reporting practices, emphasizing that transparently reporting zero revenue is better than hiding the truth.
* [ ] Verify the accuracy of revenue calculations, checking for the "GMV trap" involving rebates or cashback.

### Retention & Cohort Analysis
* [ ] Analyze monthly user cohorts to assess business stickiness and determine if retention curves are successfully flattening out.
* [ ] Evaluate Net Dollar Retention (NDR) against industry benchmarks (>100% organic growth, 110-120% mature B2B SaaS, 125-150% early-stage B2B SaaS).
* [ ] Provide a strong recommendation to halt rapid scaling and focus on customer interviews to fix product issues if NDR is below 100%.

### Unit Economics Optimization
* [ ] Review Gross Margin calculations to ensure all true COGS, including realistic long-term AI model costs, are factored in.
* [ ] Identify risks associated with negative unit economics or operational business models with inherently low margins.
* [ ] Advise the founder on strategies to flip negative unit economics to profitability (e.g., bringing tech in-house, adding paid features).
* [ ] Ensure the founder halts rapid scaling efforts until negative gross margins are successfully fixed.
